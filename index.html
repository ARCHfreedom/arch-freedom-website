Our Recent Craftsmanship

Craftsmanship Identity

The ARCH Freedom Signature

Every structure we build carries a mark of distinction. Not a sticker. Not a stamp from a catalog. A permanent, hand-applied brand — burned into natural wood or precision-mounted in brushed stainless steel on composite. It is our commitment to the build, left behind for the life of the structure.

Natural Wood

Electric Brand — All-Wood Builds

Every structure built entirely from natural wood receives our AF monogram burned directly into the post face with a precision electric branding iron. Cedar, pine, hardwood — the mark becomes part of the material itself, deepening in character as the structure weathers and ages. No adhesive. No hardware. Just the wood and the mark.

2.5″ × 2.5″  •  Electric Brand Iron  •  All-Wood Builds
Composite Deck

Brushed Stainless Placard — Composite & Plastic-Accent Builds

Any build incorporating composite decking, PVC trim, or plastic-accent materials receives our full signature as a precision-engraved brushed stainless steel nameplate. Four countersunk screws. Fully weatherproof. Where a brand iron cannot bond to the material, the placard speaks with the same permanence — mounted to last the life of the structure.

Brushed 304 Stainless  •  Engraved  •  Composite & Plastic-Accent Builds
